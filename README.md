## Core Thesis in Three Principles

1. **The Unsigned Universe and the 4-Cell Epistemic Lattice**: Unlike a closed game or simulation where a designer explicitly signs off on the reward and truth function, our universe never officially declares or signs any law as eternal, final truth; it only returns working outcomes when we act upon it. Science classifies phenomena within this unsigned universe into four cells: **① Decidable Observation (True / False)**, **② Undecidable Observation (structural indeterminacy such as $0/0$)**, **③ Known Unknown**, and **④ Unknown Unknown (`etc.`)**. Because `etc.` covers every remaining possibility, the four-cell *frame* is logically complete (MECE), yet inside an unsigned universe its *interior* can never be 100% filled.
2. **The Asymptote ($0.999\dots$) and the Axiomatic Leap ($=1$)**: Before an unsigned universe, both humans and any future superintelligence are bound by the same law: observation only ever approaches the **asymptote ($0.999\dots$)**, never absolute $1$. Although an unending approach ($0.999\dots$) and a completed truth ($1$) are distinct, inquiring minds establish a shared **Axiom (promise)** declaring $0.999\dots \equiv 1$ once cross-validated across diverse perspectives so that intelligence can step onto that stone and begin the next level of inquiry.
3. **The Circulation of Difference ($x_i \neq x_j$) and Sameness ($1 = 1$)**: Examined down to microscopic states and spacetime coordinates $(x, y, z, t)$, no two entities in the physical world are identical—the essence of the physical world is pervasive **Difference**. Complete, non-asymptotic **Sameness ($1 = 1$)** holds strictly as truth only inside our shared **axioms and language**. Even the digital world of `0`s and `1`s rests on the two deepest foundations of all information: **There is Difference ($0 \neq 1$)** and **There is Sameness ($1 = 1$)**. Genuine growth is the unending circulation: **Difference $\to$ Agreed Sameness (Axiom & Word) $\to$ Discovery of New Difference $\to$ Wider Sameness**.
